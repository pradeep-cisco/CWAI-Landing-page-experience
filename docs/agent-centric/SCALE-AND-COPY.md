# Option 4 — scale and copy iteration

Updated: 2026-11-24. Implemented in Option 4 only. Earlier explorations and their drafts remain unchanged.

## Outcome

Supervise agent-owned work across a large WAN: recognize consequential work, review the decisions requiring delegated authority, and start bounded investigations without reading every job.

The three design questions remain. Visible headings are **Needs review** (what should I do?), **Jobs** (what happened and what agents are doing?), and **Attention** (suggested next steps and relevant actions).

## Presentation scenario

The deterministic demonstration contains 10,000 registered devices across eight domains, 168 jobs, 112 open issues and six separate change reviews at arrival. Counts derive from the records. This is presentation data, not a live controller connection or a device-capacity benchmark.

- Domain inventory totals 10,000. Current observations cover 9,872 devices; the remaining 128 are awaiting data. Inventory and observation coverage do not establish health.
- One issue may contain jobs owned by different agents. Devices, issues, jobs and decisions are distinct objects.
- An event archive access prerequisite blocks historical analysis in 18 investigations across three domains. The shared dependency does not establish a common cause for their network symptoms.
- Existing route correction, independent configuration assessment and source-dependent investigation retain their own state and evidence.

## Landing and inspection

**Needs review** shows three scoped decisions and the actual total. View all reviews opens the complete queue. Each proposal has its own version, target, exact change, evidence, authority and verification plan. There is no blanket approval.

**Jobs** shows four representative groups: the principal routing correction, shared archive dependency, reachability investigations and change verification. Other groups and every underlying job remain available through View all jobs. Domain or agent filters adjust the view; reviews outside it remain discoverable. Search includes issue identifiers, job identifiers, devices, scope, agent and status. The workspace shows 12 jobs per page and supports running, waiting, review and completed states.

**Attention** separates optional suggestions from required decisions. Compact capabilities lead to explicit scope selection. Investigation and configuration assessment support declared domain scopes; verification requires evidence of completion in the exact change scope. The landing does not claim global readiness from one router's prerequisites. Agent assignment controls remain collapsed.

## Interaction and motion

- Supporting observations transition between a finite set of supplied facts. The main checkpoint and list order stay stable. Group observations name their individual target.
- Hover, keyboard focus, offscreen state, hidden document, reduced motion or interrupted observations pause supporting transitions. A Pause updates action is also available. This never pauses agents.
- A submitted request waits for acknowledgement. Acknowledgement establishes assessment in progress, not a result or production change.
- Additional change reviews distinguish Decision submitted, Approval confirmed and prerequisite checking. They do not invent execution or verification. The original route scenario retains its separate execution and verification stages.
- A selected job remains reachable while results update. Native Magnetic drawers provide evidence, related network context and recorded explanations.
- Quiet snapshots describe assessed scopes; interrupted snapshots retain last-known records and disable new starts and approvals. Separate preview fixtures retain their own requested work.

## Copy decisions

Use sentence case, concrete subjects and specific actions. Prefer View details, Review change, Choose scope, Open job, Check configuration and Verify change. Remove repeated Agent update labels, card-level job identifiers and redundant Suggested / Optional labels. Keep precise authority and change limits in the review flow.

## Preservation

This iteration adds an isolated scale model to the Option 4 story model. It does not change Options 1–3, archived explorations, the WAN map, shared navigation or their model state. The floating exploration switcher remains the route between options.
