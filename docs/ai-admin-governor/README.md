## Current direction

The user's clarification is the central job: **“Supervise the AI workforce.”**

The AI Admin's daily landing experience centers on agent activity, pending approvals, remediation work, trust/compliance, automated actions, escalations, and agent health. Policy administration, evaluation, production eligibility, and detailed audit support that daily supervision through relevant inspection and controls.

> I know what the AI workforce is doing, whether it is operating reliably and within bounds, where my attention is needed, and what resulted from its actions.

Crosswork AI is the software governor: it coordinates and follows permitted work, handles dependencies/recovery, enforces supported boundaries, and verifies results. The human supervisor makes the precise decisions that require their authority and retains awareness of the shared workforce.
